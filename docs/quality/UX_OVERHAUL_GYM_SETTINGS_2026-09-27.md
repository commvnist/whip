### Chart interaction fixture reconciliation

The final Progress drill-down fixture initially failed because its test scroll targeted a child in a tall chart item. Diagnostics showed the chart-point chip starting at 848 dp, below the list's 826 dp bottom; the nearest horizontal-scroll ancestor did not change its vertical position. A real vertical swipe exposed the chip at 694 dp, after which the strict visibility assertion, real touch selection, source dialog, View Workout action and focused History assertions all passed in [native run 7](../../build/ux-overhaul-native-7.log).
