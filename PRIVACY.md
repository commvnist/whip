# Privacy Policy for Whip

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Whip (`commvne.com.whip.app`) is a local-first Android application. Whip does
not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use personal data for advertising or
profiling. The app has no account system, analytics service, advertising SDK,
crash-reporting service, or developer-operated server.

## Data processed on your device

Information you enter—including tasks, habits, goals, measurements, workouts,
preferences, and history—is stored locally in the app's private storage.

If you choose to enable an optional feature, Whip may process the following
information locally:

- Health Connect data types you explicitly authorize, to power a habit, goal,
  or measurement you configure. Resulting records may be stored in Whip's local
  database, but are not sent to the developer or another service by Whip.
- Notification settings and schedules needed to deliver reminders locally.

You can deny or revoke optional permissions in Android Settings. Core tracking
features continue to work without Health Connect access.

## Backups and exports

Whip only creates backups or exports in a location you select or explicitly
configure. These files remain under your control. Once a file is exported from
the app's private storage, deleting Whip does not delete that external copy.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear Whip's storage, or
uninstall the app. Whip has no remote copy to retain or delete. You must delete
any separately exported backup or CSV files from their chosen storage location.

## Children

Whip does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published in this file with a revised effective date.
The repository history provides a record of prior versions.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the
[Whip GitHub repository](https://github.com/commvnist/whip/issues). Do not put
sensitive personal information in a public issue.
