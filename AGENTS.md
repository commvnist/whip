# VERA-Codex activation and safety

Use the repository skill at `.agents/skills/vera-codex/SKILL.md` as `$vera-codex` before substantive work on every non-trivial software development, diagnosis, testing, or code-review request. Follow its task contract, risk gates, routing, verification, repair, review, and stopping rules. Keep the central parent responsible for scope, integration, and final acceptance.

Standing authorization covers only reversible, in-scope agent work: bounded read-only discovery, local edits, and local checks required by the skill. Preserve pre-existing changes and obey the user's explicit scope and constraints.

Standing authorization does not cover destructive or irreversible operations, production actions, credentials or secrets, billing, publication, deployment, or other external effects. Obtain explicit user approval at the final actionable boundary before any such effect. Applicable higher-priority instructions and other triggered skills remain controlling.
